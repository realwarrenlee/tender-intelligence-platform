## Evaluation method

Use evidence at the level it supports. Public and synthetic materials can establish plausible scenarios and controlled behavior; representative customer cases with actual reviewers are needed for workflow fit and task performance. An authorised live pilot can measure operational coverage and adoption. Neither a fictional test nor one known real case establishes general customer performance.

For each case, preserve source provenance, file inventory and hashes, versions, effective dates, expected source anchors, reviewer and rubric. Separate development from untouched evaluation cases; retire an exposed holdout and replace it. Record missing or inaccessible evidence and make no unsupported eligibility claim.

Compare manual work, a configured general agent and this workspace on the same accessible inputs and an equivalent acceptable deliverable. Include preparation, retrieval, review, correction, coordination and approval, plus incomplete and failed cases. Record setup separately with an explicit amortisation assumption. Use independent reviewers and representative roles; counterbalance order where practical.

Human-effort saving is `(manual minutes - assisted minutes) / manual minutes`. Report per-case active minutes and elapsed time separately. Do not substitute generation latency for human effort or treat unresolved fields as correctly extracted requirements.

## Measures and control gates

- Report critical dates and mandatory conditions as correct, missing or wrong against an independent key. Measure requirement recall and precision separately, with a declared matching rule.
- Assess claim-to-source support and semantic correctness, unsupported statements and invented citations. Unknown, expired or conflicting evidence must remain unresolved; absent evidence is not proof of failure or compliance.
- Account for every supplied file, including unreadable or partial sources. A silently missed critical condition fails its test scenario.
- Check role and workspace isolation, current-version decisions, amendment invalidation, duplicate requests and job recovery. Human approval must not be granted by model output.
- For future commercial or proposal work, compare supported calculations against an independent arithmetic key and identify units, validity, exclusions and unsupported workbook areas. Accepted factual claims require evidence or an explicitly owned human-approved assumption.
- Record failures, corrections, latency, actual usage-derived cost and retries. Passing a finite controlled set does not promise zero errors on future documents.

## Before a pilot

Agree the scope, supported service lines and representative case set with the customer. Establish current Company and bidding-entity evidence, named reviewers and decision delegation, authorised source access, and requirements for hosting, retention, model processing and permissions. Measure an equivalent manual baseline and assisted work with independent review.

For broader commercial work, obtain representative quotations, workbook rules and acceptance criteria. If source access, evidence ownership or equivalent measurement is unavailable, narrow the claim and continue with controlled evaluation rather than treating the pilot gate as passed.
