# AI-Enabled Business Development and Tender Intelligence Platform for Facilities Management

## Challenge Owner

CBM Pte Ltd (CBM) is a leading integrated facilities management company with approximately 2,100 employees serving clients across Singapore and the region. Established in 1971 as a subsidiary of City Developments Limited (CDL), CBM provides a comprehensive range of facilities management services including engineering, environmental services, security, project management, managing agent services, training and consultancy.

## Context

Business development teams operate in an increasingly competitive environment where opportunities are sourced across multiple procurement portals, client networks and marketing channels. CBM actively participates in tenders across GeBIZ, SESAMi, TenderBoard and Ariba, and the volume and complexity of opportunities continues to grow. Yet the processes supporting the full business development lifecycle remain largely manual and fragmented, placing significant strain on the team.

Teams spend considerable effort monitoring multiple portals, reviewing large volumes of tender documents, assessing bid suitability, coordinating internal reviews and consolidating information from disparate sources. Critical knowledge including historical proposals, bid outcomes, lessons learnt, pricing approaches and subject matter expertise is dispersed across systems, documents, emails and individual teams, making it difficult to apply consistently or build upon over time.

Beyond tender monitoring, the broader lifecycle spanning opportunity qualification, proposal preparation, compliance management, review and approvals, and post-bid assessment relies heavily on manual effort and individual experience, resulting in inefficiencies, inconsistent decision-making and missed opportunities for organisational learning.

CBM is running this challenge to find an AI-enabled solution that transforms these activities from a manual, fragmented and experience-dependent process into a connected, intelligence-led workflow, enabling greater productivity, better decision-making and continuous improvement across the business development and tender lifecycle.

## Challenge Statement

How might we automate and connect CBM's end-to-end tender lifecycle, from opportunity discovery and bid qualification through to proposal development, submission and post-bid review, enabling teams to work more efficiently, make better decisions and improve bid outcomes?

## What Are We Looking For?

CBM is seeking an innovative technology partner to design and develop an AI-enabled tender intelligence solution, scoped as a focused prototype with clear extensibility into a broader business development lifecycle platform. The solution should automate and connect priority activities across tender discovery, document analysis, opportunity qualification, proposal development, and review and submission workflows.

### Tender Discovery and Monitoring

- **AI-Powered Tender Discovery, Monitoring and Workflow Visibility.** To automatically monitor, consolidate and analyse tender opportunities from multiple procurement platforms and relevant sources, identifying, categorising and prioritising opportunities based on predefined business criteria, service offerings and historical success factors. The solution should provide dashboards, notifications and real-time workflow visibility enabling users to track tender opportunities, assessment status, submission progress, review outcomes and key deadlines from a single interface.

- **AI-Powered Tender Screening and Tracking.** To automatically screen and monitor tender opportunities across platforms such as GeBIZ, SESAMi, TenderBoard and Ariba, assess their relevance based on predefined criteria, and track key information including tender scope, closing dates, eligibility requirements and submission status to support faster tender identification and decision-making.

- **Marketing Intelligence.** To analyse marketing campaigns, customer engagement activities and industry trends to identify emerging opportunities and evaluate marketing effectiveness, generating recommendations to support opportunity targeting and business development prioritisation.

### Tender Assessment and Bid Decision

- **Tender Document Extraction and Summarisation.** To automatically extract, structure and summarise key tender information including scope, eligibility requirements, submission deadlines, compliance obligations and evaluation criteria, presented clearly to facilitate faster understanding and decision-making.

- **Opportunity Assessment, Project Costing and Bid Qualification.** To assess opportunity suitability based on business criteria, service capabilities, commercial considerations and historical bid performance. Where feasible, the solution should support preliminary project costing and generate actionable bid or no-bid recommendations supported by clear rationale and relevant supporting information, including department or service-line routing suggestions, to enable users to make informed decisions. Recommendations should be explainable and transparent, with human review retained at all decision points.

- **Advanced Cost-Benefit Evaluation.** To evaluate the cost, effort and resource investment required to pursue a tender against its potential value and commercial viability. Where sufficient historical data is available, it should be applied to the success probability modelling to inform bid or no-bid decisions and opportunity prioritisation.

### RFQ Process

- **AI-Assisted RFQ and Quotation Management.** To automatically analyse tender documents and generate accurate responses to vendor RFQ questions based on the relevant tender requirements, clauses and specifications, reducing manual checking and ensuring consistent communication.

- **AI-Assisted Quotation Comparison and Verification.** To automatically compare vendor quotations against tender requirements, identify price differences, missing items, scope discrepancies and potential errors, presented clearly to facilitate faster cost analysis and decision-making.

- **AI-Assisted RFQ Follow-Up and Quotation Tracking.** To automatically send follow-up emails to vendors for outstanding RFQs and track the quotation status, analysing which services or vendors have yet to submit their quotations, presented clearly to facilitate timely follow-up and quotation management.

### Costing

- **Costing Template Verification.** To automatically cross-check the costing template in Excel by validating formulas, calculations, quantities, rates and totals, and identifying discrepancies or potential errors to ensure accurate and reliable tender costing.

- **AI-Powered Costing Summary and Column Mapping.** To automatically analyse the summary tab and client price breakdown, identify the relevant costing information and correctly link the corresponding columns, reducing manual data mapping and minimising errors in the costing template.

- **AI-Powered Historical Price Estimation.** To analyse previous vendor quotations with similar scopes and use historical pricing data to estimate reasonable prices for services where current vendor quotations have not been received, supporting more accurate and timely tender costing.

### Proposal Development and Submission

- **Automated Routing and Workflow Orchestration.** To automatically route tender opportunities to the appropriate service lines, departments or subject matter experts based on tender requirements, facilitating collaboration, review workflows and approvals to reduce manual coordination effort.

- **AI-Assisted Proposal Development, Knowledge Reuse and Change Detection.** To provide intelligent access to historical proposals, lessons learnt, best practices and organisational knowledge repositories, supporting proposal preparation through content recommendations, compliance checks and knowledge retrieval to improve submission quality and reduce duplication of effort. The solution should also compare original tender documents against subsequently issued corrigenda, automatically identifying changes, additions and revisions and highlighting their impact to help users remain aligned with the latest tender requirements throughout the proposal development process.

- **Mobilisation Planning.** To support mobilisation planning following a successful award by identifying key deliverables, resource requirements, operational dependencies and critical milestones, improving coordination across teams and providing visibility into mobilisation readiness and progress.

- **Enterprise Systems Integration.** To integrate with internal systems and relevant business platforms to support data sharing, knowledge retrieval and workflow automation across the business development and tender lifecycle.

### Vendor Engagement

- **Vendor Engagement and Relationship Analysis.** To analyse vendor engagement based on quotation response rates, response time, follow-up frequency, pricing competitiveness and past collaboration records, helping to assess vendor commitment and relationship strength for future RFQ and tender opportunities.

### Post-Bid Review and Continuous Learning

- **Post-Bid Review and Continuous Learning.** To capture and analyse historical bid outcomes, evaluation feedback and lessons learnt to identify factors contributing to success or failure. Insights generated should be fed back into the system to improve future tender assessments, opportunity qualification, proposal quality and bid or no-bid decision-making over time, supporting continuous organisational learning across the business development function.

## Overall Performance Requirements

- **Integrated Data, Intelligence and User Experience.** Aggregate tender data from multiple sources and platforms through varying access methods including email alerts, downloadable documents, structured data exports and manually uploaded files, with the ability to process and extract structured information from diverse document formats. Apply AI and machine learning to assess tender suitability, generate bid or no-bid recommendations, identify patterns from historical bid data, and enable capture, organisation and retrieval of organisational knowledge including past proposals, lessons learnt and templates. Deliver these capabilities through configurable alerts and notifications covering new opportunities, deadlines, document changes and workflow updates, via an intuitive interface that requires minimal training and supports adoption across CBM's business development and service-line teams.

- **Scalable, Configurable and Secure Architecture.** Support deployment across multiple business units, service lines and users with configurable workflows and business rules, future integration with additional data sources and enterprise systems, and compliance with relevant data protection and security requirements to ensure sensitive commercial information is handled appropriately.

- **Prototype Scope, Assumptions and Constraints.** Where live platform integration is not feasible, testing may use sandbox or controlled datasets. Solvers should clearly state where test environments have been used, how the solution would transition to live data at full deployment, and all assumptions, dependencies and constraints relating to data access, authentication and data availability.

There are no restrictions on the geographical location of the challenge solvers who may choose to apply to this challenge. However, the prototype must be demonstrated in Singapore.

## Metrics of Success

- **Business Development Productivity and Decision Speed.** Achieve a 30–50% reduction in time spent on manual tender monitoring, opportunity screening and administrative review during the prototype phase, with a longer-term target of greater than 50% reduction following full deployment. Through AI-assisted analysis, teams should be able to assess tender opportunities and reach bid or no-bid decisions faster and more consistently, freeing capacity for higher-value strategic and client engagement activities.

- **Opportunity Identification and Routing.** Increase the volume of relevant tender opportunities identified and routed to the right teams, with a measurable reduction in missed opportunities.

- **Proposal Efficiency, Knowledge Reuse and Business Outcomes.** Reduce proposal preparation effort through automated content recommendations, knowledge reuse and AI-assisted document analysis, with increased capture and application of lessons learnt and historical bid data. These efficiencies should translate into improved tender win rates and proposal quality through better opportunity selection, data-driven recommendations and continuous learning, measured over a one-to-two-year deployment horizon.

- **GenAI Accuracy and MVP Performance Indicators.** During UAT, AI-generated outputs including tender summaries, requirement extractions, categorisation, routing recommendations and bid or no-bid rationales should achieve at least 70% accuracy against agreed human review criteria, with discrepancies used to refine prompt design, model configuration and workflow rules. Broader prototype success should be measured through indicators including time to identify and shortlist relevant tenders, time to generate a structured tender summary, accuracy of extracted tender fields, relevance of recommendations, reduction in duplicate manual effort, user adoption across business development and service-line teams, and number of sources monitored.

## Possible Use Cases

### 1. Faster Tender Qualification and Decision-Making

Jason, CBM's Business Development Executive, is responsible for identifying and qualifying incoming tender opportunities across multiple procurement platforms. On any given day, he is simultaneously tracking new postings on GeBIZ, SESAMi and TenderBoard, downloading and reviewing lengthy tender documents, and coordinating with service line leads to determine whether CBM has the capability and capacity to bid. Each tender review can take several hours, and with opportunities arriving continuously, Jason frequently risks missing relevant tenders or making rushed qualification decisions under time pressure. Critical context, such as whether CBM has bid on similar work before and what the outcome was, is rarely at his fingertips and requires manual searching across shared drives and email threads.

With the solution in place, Jason logs in to a single dashboard that consolidates new tender opportunities from all monitored platforms, ranked by relevance against CBM's service capabilities and historical bid profile. He selects a tender and the system automatically generates a structured summary covering scope, eligibility requirements, submission deadlines, compliance obligations and evaluation criteria. A preliminary bid or no-bid recommendation is surfaced alongside supporting rationale drawn from past bid outcomes and current capacity indicators. Jason reviews the recommendation, adds his own assessment, and routes the opportunity to the relevant service line leads with a single action. What previously took the better part of a day is completed in under an hour, allowing Jason to process a higher volume of opportunities with greater consistency and confidence.

### 2. Streamlined Cross-Functional Collaboration

Sarah, CBM's Business Development Manager, oversees the evaluation and pursuit of shortlisted tender opportunities across multiple service lines. Once a tender has been qualified, she is responsible for engaging the relevant subject matter experts, collecting their inputs, consolidating feedback and coordinating the internal review and approval process before a bid decision is made. In practice, this involves extensive email exchanges, repeated follow-ups and manual tracking of who has reviewed what and when. With multiple tenders in flight simultaneously and service line leads managing their own operational priorities, delays in the review cycle are common and accountability is difficult to maintain. Important inputs are frequently buried in email threads or missing entirely when the deadline arrives.

With the solution in place, Sarah receives a routed tender opportunity with a pre-populated review workflow that automatically identifies the relevant stakeholders based on the tender's service scope and assigns review tasks with clear deadlines. Each reviewer receives a notification with the tender summary and a structured input template, and Sarah can track completion status in real time from her dashboard. The system sends automated reminders to reviewers who have not responded and flags outstanding inputs as the submission deadline approaches. Sarah's role shifts from manually chasing responses to reviewing a consolidated assessment and making an informed bid decision, significantly reducing coordination overhead and improving the consistency and timeliness of CBM's tender evaluation process.

### 3. Efficient Proposal Development and Knowledge Reuse

David, CBM's Proposal Lead, is responsible for preparing tender submissions from end to end, from drafting the technical response and methodology to compiling supporting documents and ensuring full compliance with submission requirements. His core challenge is that every proposal is largely built from scratch. Relevant content from past submissions, including company credentials, methodology write-ups, case studies and pricing rationale, is scattered across shared drives, individual laptops and email archives with no consistent structure or tagging. Locating and adapting reusable content can consume as much time as writing new content, and when tender corrigenda are issued mid-process, identifying what has changed and updating the proposal accordingly adds further pressure to an already tight timeline.

With the solution in place, David begins proposal preparation by entering the tender requirements into the system, which retrieves and recommends relevant content from CBM's centralised knowledge repository, including past proposals, methodology templates, case studies and lessons learnt from similar bids. The system flags content that may need updating for compliance or relevance and highlights sections where new content is required. When a corrigendum is issued, the solution automatically compares it against the original tender document, surfaces a clear summary of changes and identifies the sections of the draft proposal that are affected. David's role shifts from manually searching for content and tracking document changes to reviewing, refining and approving a well-structured draft, enabling him to produce higher-quality submissions in significantly less time.
