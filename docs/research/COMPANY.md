# CBM Pte. Ltd. — Bid qualification knowledge base

Maintained as of 28 October 2026. Legal entity: CBM PTE. LTD., UEN 197100922N. This is the company reference for assessing new tender opportunities. It combines verified public observations, published company statements and explicitly labelled analysis. Internal operating and commercial facts that CBM has not supplied remain unknown.

## 1. What the evidence says about CBM

CBM is a credible company to consider for integrated facilities management, managing-agent services, building-services maintenance, selected building improvement works and car-park operations. This assessment is based on the scope of its public-sector award records, its listed BCA registrations and its published services. It is a scope-fit conclusion, not a claim that CBM can meet every condition of a new tender. The most useful starting references are the SFA wholesale-centre IFM award, the two distinct MHA appointments, MSF social-facility FM, HDB managing-agent work, PA community-property contracts, and the NParks ACMV/chiller project. [S01, S06, S07, S15]

The evidence is strongest for a business that coordinates facilities operations and building services across different property settings. It is materially weaker for claims about available manpower, particular named specialists, current workload, completed-project performance, margins and mobilisations. The public record supports investigating an opportunity. A bid recommendation also needs the actual tender conditions and current internal evidence.

The decision has four separate parts: eligibility to participate; ability to deliver the required scope and service levels; commercial attractiveness; and authority to pursue. A favourable answer to one does not settle the others. For example, a suitable BCA workhead can support registration eligibility while a missing staffing commitment still blocks delivery confirmation. A parent-group relationship is not a performance guarantee or evidence of working capital.

## 3. How strong each type of evidence is

An official register supports the particular registration, grade or licence observation recorded there, as of the lookup date and subject to the stated expiry. It does not independently establish a tender-specific staffing plan or future renewal.

An official award record supports that a named supplier received an award on the recorded date for the published scope and amount/status. It does not by itself establish commencement, completion, satisfactory performance, final contract value, remaining capacity or permission to disclose the customer's material.

A company-published service page supports what the company says it offers. A company announcement can identify a potentially useful project, but a signed award letter, contract schedule and client reference are stronger evidence for a formal qualifying-experience requirement.

An analytical implication in this file explains why evidence may be relevant to a new tender. It is not an additional fact about CBM. In particular, possible experience with sensitive, laboratory or occupied environments must not become a claim of specific accreditations, clearance or demonstrated service levels without supporting records.

Internal confirmation should identify the person responsible, the evidence, its date, the relevant entity and the tender condition it answers. A general statement such as "we have engineers" is weaker than a named, qualified team committed to the required hours and mobilisation date. No named internal owner or approved resource commitment has been provided for this reference.

BCA's published limits for 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027 show FM01 M1 as unlimited, CW01 B1 as S$50 million, and specialist CR/ME grades L6 as unlimited, L5 as S$16 million, L3 as S$5 million and L2 as S$1.6 million. "Unlimited" here describes the published registry-grade tendering limit. It does not mean unlimited delivery resources, a right to undertake any regulated activity, or exemption from a buyer's additional conditions. Determine which workhead and valuation basis the actual tender uses before comparing a price with a limit. Do not compare a multi-year FM award amount mechanically with an unrelated specialist-workhead cap.

Compare validity with the date that the tender requires: closing, award, commencement or the service period, as specified. A tender closing after a listed expiry needs a fresh registry/certificate check and any acceptable renewal evidence. An intention to renew is not an issued renewal.

BCA describes FM01 as covering IFM and managing-agent services involving at least two distinct FM services. Its guidance distinguishes building maintenance, M&E maintenance, security, cleaning, landscaping and pest control, and recognises that services may be delivered directly or centrally managed through outsourcing. That makes FM01 relevant to an integrated package; the buyer's exact workhead and subcontracting conditions remain decisive. [S02, S19]

BCA registration alone is not a substitute for a current cleaning-business, security-agency or other activity-specific licence where the work requires one. This research has not established the complete, current licence/certificate pack for the proposed bidding and delivery entities. ISO certificates, bizSAFE status, Progressive Wage accreditation, GeBIZ supply heads, insurance and specialist licences must be checked against actual tender requirements rather than inferred from a group logo or registration code.

## 8. How to match a new opportunity to this evidence

### 8.1 Scope fit

An integrated building/estate FM tender should first be compared with SFA, MSF, MHA and the relevant PA/HDB records. Establish whether the buyer is procuring IFM operation, a managing agent, direct specialist maintenance, project works or a combination. The same property can have several separate appointments; the existence of one role does not prove experience in all the others.

A recurring ACMV or multi-trade M&E tender should first be compared with PA's technical-maintenance records and the relevant engineering registrations. A chiller/ACMV alteration project should additionally examine NParks. A general FM record is weaker evidence for a highly specific OEM, equipment-capacity, commissioning or certification condition.

A building-repair/cyclical-works opportunity should examine SLA, Changi-Simei, the works component of MHA West Zone and the older Town Council result. A managing-agent fee contract cannot automatically satisfy a minimum construction-works value or a main-contractor completion criterion.

A standalone parking opportunity should examine the two government rows naming CBM Pte. Ltd. Affiliate parking announcements may support a permitted partner route, but require entity and delivery checks. Security, specialist sustainability, pest-control and overseas opportunities should similarly receive entity/licence-specific review rather than a blanket group-capability pass.

### 8.2 Qualifying experience

For each proposed comparable contract, establish the entity; client; actual scope; sites and asset scale; CBM's role; start and actual completion dates or accepted ongoing status; value on the buyer's required basis; delivery/performance evidence; and the client contact/reuse permission. Link these facts to the exact new criterion.

"Two comparable completed contracts in the last three years" cannot be met simply by finding two award rows. A contract can have an old award date but a qualifying recent completion; a recent award may still be ongoing. The tender may also distinguish a prime contractor from a managing agent, subcontractor or group member. Apply its actual definition.

Where two source records may concern overlapping appointments, explain the relationship and seek the project/lot documents. Do not improve a count by treating a company announcement, award row and client reference for one project as three projects.

### 8.3 Staffing, mobilisation and service levels

A delivery confirmation needs the required number of people, qualifications, named/key-person approvals where applicable, coverage hours, relief/leave arrangements, location, start date and an approved availability commitment. Confirm supervisors, standby response and subcontractor dependencies as required by the actual scope.

The public statement about a control centre does not prove a buyer's fifteen-minute response requirement, a particular Saturday shift or two dedicated technicians. Historical employment or a recruitment advert does not establish available capacity. None of the sources in this file establishes a tender-specific roster.

### 8.4 Commercial and contractual fit

Public award amounts do not establish CBM's cost base, charge-out rates, target margin, profitability or current borrowing capacity. Before a commercial recommendation, establish the price structure, service boundaries, wage assumptions, equipment/capital needs, subcontract quotations, mobilisation cost, cash-flow exposure, bonds/guarantees, insurance requirements and any material liabilities.

Internal targets for contract size, margin, geography, customer type, risk appetite and approval thresholds have not been supplied. They must not be invented as CBM policy. An unknown internal margin target is a commercial-review question; it is not by itself proof that a mandatory tender qualification has failed.

### 8.5 Submission readiness

Separate company capability from producing the required submission. Check the actual closing date/timezone, portal/account requirements, compulsory briefing and attendance record, clarifications/addenda, forms, declarations, signatures, pricing schedule, method statement, mobilisation plan and required attachments.

## 10. Applying this knowledge in a bid review

The output should identify what the buyer requires, the particular evidence supporting each finding, whether that evidence applies to CBM Pte. Ltd. and the required date/scope, and the specific missing fact. Keep scope-fit analysis separate from a confirmed eligibility/delivery finding.

A qualification recommendation of pursue can be supported when the relevant mandatory qualification/delivery conditions and critical tender facts are resolved with evidence current for the required dates. Commercial sign-off and the recorded human pursuit decision are separate. A recommendation does not submit a bid or record an authorised company decision.

Hold means a material fact, mandatory qualification, resource commitment or required evidence is unresolved. Identify the smallest concrete request that would resolve it. It is more useful to ask for the two client-endorsed completion records or the approved Saturday rota than to ask a vague question about whether CBM is capable.

A qualification recommendation of decline needs an evidenced failure of an actual mandatory requirement. A human may separately make an authorised commercial no-bid decision. Do not treat absence from this public research as proof that CBM lacks a capability. Conversely, a general public capability claim cannot erase a verified grade, licence or tender-condition mismatch.

### Example A — a potentially suitable IFM bid

A hypothetical tender requires FM01 M1 and two similar completed contracts within a specified recent period. The observed registry entry supports investigating the registration condition, subject to the tender's required date. SFA, MSF and the appropriate MHA/PA records provide promising project leads. The completed-contract condition remains unresolved until actual completion and scope/value evidence is available. Good initial fit is compatible with a hold on missing mandatory evidence.

### Example B — a workhead-specific mismatch

A hypothetical standalone landscaping tender expressly requires FM03 L2. The observed CBM entry is FM03 L1. FM01 M1 is not an automatic substitute. Confirm the live entry and any permitted qualification route; if the mandatory mismatch remains and no allowed route satisfies it, record that specific failure. Do not portray a grade-upgrade intention as current registration.

### Example C — a sensitive-site staffing commitment

A hypothetical buyer requires named, qualified technicians on fixed shifts with appropriate clearances. An MHA award and a published control-centre capability are relevant background, but do not identify the proposed people, availability or clearance. Resolve those exact facts before confirming delivery. A missing staffing answer is not evidence that the historical project did not occur.

### Example D — a specialised laboratory requirement

A hypothetical opportunity involves laboratory ACMV work. The NParks record is worth examining because its stated scope concerns ACMV/chiller works associated with a BSL-3 setting. It cannot establish accredited laboratory operation, biosafety certification or an identical commissioned system. The recommendation depends on the exact engineering criterion and the project documents/qualified team available to meet it.

## 11. Maintaining this company knowledge

The public research date is 28 October 2026; it is not the issue or effective date of every underlying document. Recheck registers and limits against each tender's dates.
